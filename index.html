SpareKey

The cleaner from two years ago. The ex-roommate's spare set. The dog walker's gate code. Most people have handed out access to their home a dozen times - and couldn't list who still has it.

SpareKey is a ledger for your own front door. Log every key copy, door code and remote you hand out, see at a glance what is still out there (sorted by how long it's been), and mark things returned when they come back.

No account, no cloud - everything stays in your browser.
